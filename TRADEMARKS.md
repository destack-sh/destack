## Allowed

You can use the Destack name without asking to:

- Refer to Destack truthfully, such as "built with Destack", "runs on Destack" or "an app for Destack".
- Redistribute unmodified Destack releases under the Destack name.
- Write about, teach and review Destack.
- Link to [destack.sh](https://destack.sh) with the Destack logo.

## Not allowed

You need written permission to:

- Call a modified version, fork or hosted service "Destack", or ship it with the Destack logo.
- Use "Destack" in the name of a product, company, package or domain in a way that suggests it is official.
- Suggest that Symbol Industries makes, endorses or certifies your product.

Modified versions use their own name and logo, and can say they are "based on Destack".
